Governance Commitment

VahanSathi (operated by UK Academe) maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), and any form of minor endangerment.

We are strictly committed to enforcing Google Play Developer Program Policies, the Information Technology Act (India), and international legal standards protecting child safety across all mobile services and digital touchpoints.

01

Statement of Commitment

Our organization actively works to eliminate online child exploitation. We implement preventive technical safeguards, maintain continuous operational monitoring, and strictly mandate that all features, content updates, and third-party interactions comply with international minor protection standards.

02

Prohibited Conduct & Content

We strictly prohibit any conduct, media transmission, or user interaction connected to VahanSathi that involves:

Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
Strict prohibition against storing, displaying, or transmitting visual depictions of minor exploitation.
Grooming & Luring
Zero tolerance for contacting, soliciting, or grooming minors for sexually explicit conduct.
Sextortion & Blackmail
Prohibition against coercing, blackmailing, or threatening minors using explicit material.
Child Trafficking
Strict prohibition against advertising, promoting, or facilitating child trafficking networks.
03

App Architecture & Technical Safeguards

Although VahanSathi operates primarily as an informative utility application (providing RTO exam practice, traffic sign guides, vehicle information, and process workflows):

No Unmoderated UGC: The application does not provide public chat rooms, social feeds, or unmoderated media uploading features where explicit content could be shared.
Minimal Data Collection: We enforce privacy-first data handling standards to ensure minors using the app for educational prep are completely isolated from unauthorized tracking or contact.
04

Incident Escalation & Law Enforcement

In the event that any potential CSAE violation or security concern is identified:

  1. STEP 1 Immediate Intervention: Associated accounts, access tokens, or reported content will be suspended and purged immediately from system logs.
  2. STEP 2 Law Enforcement Escalation: Mandatory escalation to authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (India).
